Privacy Policy

Effective: November 10, 2026 · App: PdfMagicTool — Split, Merge & Compress PDFs
100% offline · No accounts · No tracking

Short version

PdfMagicTool processes your PDFs entirely on your own device. We do not collect, upload, store, share, or sell any of your data — because your files never leave your device in the first place.

What stays on your device

  • Your PDF files. Splitting, merging, compression, export, and page rendering all happen locally in your browser or app shell using on-device libraries. Files are never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Your settings. Preferences (e.g. compression preset, page ranges) are remembered only in your browser's local storage (localStorage key pdfToolV1) so the app remembers them next time. Clearing site data removes them.
  • Passwords you type. If you unlock a password-protected PDF, the password lives only in the app's temporary memory while the file is open and is discarded when you load a different file or close the app.

What we collect: nothing

  • No accounts, no names, no emails, no device identifiers.
  • No analytics, no crash reporters, no advertising SDKs, no cookies for tracking.
  • No network requests except loading the app itself (and, in the web version, the brand font on first load, served from a font CDN — no personal data is sent).

Permissions (Android / installed app)

The app declares no data-access permissions. When you pick a PDF, access is granted one file at a time through your operating system's own file picker. Revoking or reinstalling the app removes everything it stored.

Children's privacy

Since no personal data is collected from anyone, there is nothing specific to children to collect either. The app is a general-purpose offline utility suitable for all ages.

Changes to this policy
